### Brady Bangasser

I like playing with distributed systems, cloud, DNS, and networking to build
reliable applications. I am a CS BS/MS student at Iowa State and a sysadmin on
the Research IT / HPC team, most interested in SRE, DevOps, platform, and
solutions architecture.

Most of what I build comes from the same itch: taking something fragile and
making it something you can depend on. That has meant operating a production HPC
cluster for real researchers, running my own hybrid cloud across on-prem and AWS,
and building encrypted distributed systems from scratch. I care about the
unglamorous parts that decide whether a system is actually reliable:
observability, failure domains, and clean automation.

My writing, projects, and resume live at **[bangasser.dev](https://www.bangasser.dev)**.
